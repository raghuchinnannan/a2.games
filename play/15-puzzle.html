Play 15 Puzzle Online Free

The 15 Puzzle is the classic sliding tile puzzle: fifteen numbered tiles, one empty space. Slide them back into order from 1 to 15.

How to play 15 Puzzle

  • Slide a tile next to the empty space into it.
  • Arrange the tiles from 1 to 15.
  • Finish in as few moves as you can.

Controls

  • Arrow keys to slide
  • Click or tap a tile

Features

  • Classic 4x4 slide puzzle
  • Quick to reset and replay
  • Keyboard and touch
  • Free to play, no download, no account
  • Runs in any modern browser, including school Chromebooks

15 Puzzle FAQ

Is every 15 Puzzle solvable?

No, only half of all possible arrangements can be solved, which is why a fair shuffle is made by sliding tiles rather than placing them at random.

How do I solve it?

Solve the top row, then the second row, then finish the bottom two rows together.
