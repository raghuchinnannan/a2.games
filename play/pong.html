How to play Pong

  • Move your paddle to hit the ball back.
  • Get the ball past your opponent to score.
  • First to the target score wins.

Controls

  • Player 1: W / S
  • Player 2: Up / Down arrow keys
  • Touch screens: drag each paddle

Features

  • Local 2-player
  • Classic retro style
  • Keyboard and touch
  • Free to play, no download, no account
  • Runs in any modern browser, including school Chromebooks

Pong FAQ

Can two people play Pong on one computer?

Yes, one uses W/S and the other uses the arrow keys.

Is it free?

Yes.

Tips?

Hit the ball with the edge of the paddle to change its angle.
