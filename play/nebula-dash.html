Play Nebula Dash Online Free

Nebula Dash is a fast space arcade game where you steer a small ship through a glowing nebula, scooping up energy orbs while rogue comets rain down. Runs get faster the longer you survive, so every second is a little harder than the last.

How to play Nebula Dash

  • Steer left and right to line up with the energy orbs.
  • Collect orbs to raise your score.
  • Avoid the comets: one hit ends the run.
  • Survive as long as you can, the field speeds up over time.

Controls

  • Left / Right arrow keys or A / D to move
  • Space to start or restart
  • On phones and tablets: tap or drag on either side of the screen

Features

  • Neon space visuals that run smoothly on low-end laptops and Chromebooks
  • Short runs that suit a quick break
  • Works with keyboard, mouse or touch
  • Free to play, no download, no account
  • Runs in any modern browser, including school Chromebooks

Nebula Dash FAQ

Is Nebula Dash free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser with no download or sign-up.

Can I play Nebula Dash on my phone?

Yes. The game has touch controls and works in mobile browsers.

How do I get a higher score in Nebula Dash?

Stay near the middle of the screen so you can move either way, and skip an orb when grabbing it would put you under a comet.
