Play Orbit Switch Online Free

Orbit Switch is a one-button arcade game. Your orb circles a core on one of two orbits, and a single tap flips it between them. Hop orbits to slip past red hazards and collect rings for points.

How to play Orbit Switch

  • Your orb moves around the core on its own.
  • Tap to jump between the inner and outer orbit.
  • Collect rings to score.
  • Touching a red hazard ends the run.

Controls

  • Space or click to switch orbit
  • Tap anywhere on touch screens

Orbit Switch FAQ

How do you play Orbit Switch?

Tap, click or press Space to swap between the two orbits, dodge the red hazards and collect rings.

Is Orbit Switch free to play online?

Yes, it is free in any modern browser on desktop or mobile.

Any tips for Orbit Switch?

Watch the hazard coming up next rather than the one you just passed, and switch a moment before it reaches you rather than early.
