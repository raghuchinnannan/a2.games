Play Brick Breaker Arcade Online Free

Brick Breaker Arcade is a Breakout-style game. Bounce the ball off your paddle, smash every brick, and catch power-ups. You have three lives.

How to play Brick Breaker Arcade

  • Move the paddle to keep the ball in play.
  • Break all the bricks to clear the level.
  • Catch falling power-ups.
  • Missing the ball costs a life; you have three.

Controls

  • Left / Right arrow keys or move the mouse
  • Drag on touch screens

Features

  • Power-ups
  • Three lives
  • Keyboard, mouse and touch controls
  • Free to play, no download, no account
  • Runs in any modern browser, including school Chromebooks
