How to play Tic Tac Toe

  • Take turns placing X and O.
  • Get three in a row to win.
  • A full board with no line is a draw.

Controls

  • Click or tap a square

Tic Tac Toe FAQ

Is Tic Tac Toe free?

Yes.

How do you always win at Tic Tac Toe?

You can't always win against perfect play, but starting in a corner gives your opponent the most chances to slip up.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes, pass the device back and forth and take turns.
