Play A2 Bros Online Free

A2 Bros is a classic 2D platform adventure made with KaboomJS. Run, jump, grab coins and power-ups, avoid spikes and enemies, and find the portal to the next level.

How to play A2 Bros

  • Run and jump through the level.
  • Collect coins and power-ups.
  • Avoid spikes and enemies, or stomp them.
  • Reach the portal to move on.

Controls

  • Left / Right arrow keys to move
  • Space to jump
  • On-screen buttons on touch screens

Features

  • Multiple levels
  • Retro platforming
  • Sound effects and power-ups
  • Free to play, no download, no account
  • Runs in any modern browser, including school Chromebooks
