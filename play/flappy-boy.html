Play Flappy Boy Online Free

Flappy Boy is a tap-to-fly game in the style of Flappy Bird. Each tap gives a little lift; gravity does the rest. Thread through the gaps and see how far you get.

How to play Flappy Boy

  • Tap to flap upward.
  • Stop tapping to fall.
  • Fly through the gaps between obstacles.
  • Hitting anything ends the run.

Controls

  • Space or click to flap
  • Tap on touch screens

Features

  • Simple one-button control
  • Quick restarts
  • Works on any device
  • Free to play, no download, no account
  • Runs in any modern browser, including school Chromebooks

Flappy Boy FAQ

Is Flappy Boy like Flappy Bird?

Yes, it uses the same tap-to-fly idea, but it is an original a2.games game.

Is it free?

Yes, free in your browser.

Tips?

Tap in a steady rhythm and aim for the lower half of each gap.
