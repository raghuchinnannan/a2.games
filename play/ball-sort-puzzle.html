Play Ball Sort Puzzle Online Free

Ball Sort Puzzle is a calm logic game. Move coloured balls between tubes until every tube holds a single colour. You can only put a ball on top of the same colour or into an empty tube.

How to play Ball Sort Puzzle

  • Tap a tube to pick up its top ball.
  • Tap another tube to drop it.
  • A ball can only go on a matching colour or into an empty tube.
  • Sort every colour into its own tube.

Controls

  • Click or tap tubes
