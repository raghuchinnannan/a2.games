Play Block Drop Online Free

Block Drop is a relaxed block puzzle. Pick a Tetris-style piece, place it on the board, and clear full rows and columns to score. There's no timer, so plan your space carefully.

How to play Block Drop

  • Choose one of the offered pieces.
  • Drag it onto the board.
  • Fill a full row or column to clear it.
  • The game ends when no piece fits.

Controls

  • Drag pieces with the mouse
  • Drag with your finger on touch screens

Features

  • No time pressure
  • Combo bonus for clearing several lines
  • Mouse and touch friendly
  • Free to play, no download, no account
  • Runs in any modern browser, including school Chromebooks

Block Drop FAQ

Is Block Drop like Tetris?

It uses Tetris-like shapes, but you place them freely instead of dropping them, similar to popular block puzzles.

Is it free?

Yes.

Tips?

Keep a 3x3 space open for big pieces and clear lines as soon as you can.
