How to play Sudoku

  • Select an empty cell.
  • Enter a digit from 1 to 9.
  • Each row, column and 3x3 box must contain every digit exactly once.
  • Finish the grid to solve the puzzle.

Controls

  • Click or tap a cell, then choose a number
  • Works with mouse or touch

How do I solve Sudoku faster?

Scan each 3x3 box for numbers that can only go in one cell, then look for rows and columns with just one or two gaps.
