Aim, shoot and pop. In Bubble Shooter you fire coloured bubbles into the cluster above; connect three or more of the same colour and they burst. Clear the board before the bubbles reach the bottom.

How to play Bubble Shooter

  • Aim with the mouse or your finger.
  • Shoot a bubble into the cluster.
  • Groups of three or more matching bubbles pop.
  • Bubbles left hanging with no support drop too.

Controls

  • Move the mouse to aim, click to shoot
  • Drag and release on touch screens

Features

  • 3D-style bubbles
  • Bank shots off the side walls
  • Relaxing, casual pace
  • Free to play, no download, no account
  • Runs in any modern browser, including school Chromebooks

Bubble Shooter FAQ

Is Bubble Shooter free?

Yes, play it free in your browser.

How do I score more in Bubble Shooter?

Pop the bubbles holding up big clusters so the rest fall at once, and use the walls for angled shots.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes, aim by dragging and release to shoot.
