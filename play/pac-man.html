A tribute to the classic maze chase: eat every pellet in the maze, stay away from the ghosts, and grab a power-up to turn the tables. This is an independent fan-made clone, free to play in your browser.

How to play Pac-Man Clone

  • Move through the maze eating pellets.
  • Avoid the ghosts, touching one costs a life.
  • Power-ups let you chase the ghosts for a short time.
  • Clear all pellets to finish the level.

Controls

  • Arrow keys to move
  • On-screen buttons or swipe on touch screens

Features

  • Retro maze arcade gameplay
  • Power-up pellets
  • Keyboard and touch controls
  • Free to play, no download, no account
  • Runs in any modern browser, including school Chromebooks

Pac-Man Clone FAQ

Is this the official Pac-Man?

No. It is an independent clone inspired by the arcade classic and is not affiliated with the Pac-Man trademark owner.

Is it free?

Yes, it is free to play in your browser.

Any tips?

Save power-ups until ghosts are close, and use corners to break their line of sight.
