How to play 2048

  • Every move slides all tiles as far as they can go in one direction.
  • Two tiles with the same number merge into their sum.
  • A new 2 or 4 appears after each move.
  • The game ends when the grid is full and no merges are left.

Controls

  • Arrow keys to slide tiles
  • Swipe on phones and tablets

What is the best 2048 strategy?

Keep your highest tile in one corner, build a descending chain along one edge, and avoid the move that pulls the big tile out of its corner.
